About Leader

Ayatollah Ali Khamenei is the supreme leader of Iran and the spiritual guide of Shia Muslims worldwide, a respected scholar who has spent his life studying and teaching Islam. He is known as a voice for justice and a true friend of the oppressed, with his firm stand for Palestine making him a symbol of hope for Muslims everywhere. In 1981, he was badly hurt in a bomb attack while leading prayers at a mosque in Tehran, where a hidden explosive injured his hand, arm, and lung; though he survived, his right hand never fully healed, a sacrifice that shows his courage and deep commitment to serving Islam even at the cost of his own life.
